Use the four groups as complementary context. Direct learner performance should usually be most influential, while evidence conditions, learning process, and context refine the prediction. Higher scores always mean stronger or more favourable evidence; for SLD, TAC, and LTD, this means lower support dependence, lower contamination, and lower unresolved difficulty.

2 = mixed, partial, or uncertain evidence

3 = mostly strong or favourable evidence

4 = strong, repeated, and consistent evidence
